# Skill: Daily Briefing

Generate a CEO-style daily briefing from the vault and save it to /Briefings/.

## Instructions

You are the AI Employee. Generate a comprehensive daily briefing for the CEO (vault owner).

**Steps:**

1. Read `AI_Employee_Vault/Company_Handbook.md` for business goals and thresholds.
2. Read `AI_Employee_Vault/Dashboard.md` for current system status.
3. Read `AI_Employee_Vault/Accounting/Bank_Transactions.md` for financial data.
4. List all files in `AI_Employee_Vault/Done/` created today to see what was completed.
5. List all files in `AI_Employee_Vault/Needs_Action/` to see what is still pending.
6. List all files in `AI_Employee_Vault/Pending_Approval/` to see what needs human action.

**Generate a briefing file** at `AI_Employee_Vault/Briefings/YYYY-MM-DD_Daily_Briefing.md` with this structure:

```markdown
---
generated: <ISO timestamp>
period: <yesterday to today>
type: daily_briefing
---

# Daily CEO Briefing — <Date>

## Executive Summary
<2-3 sentence overview of the day>

## Inbox & Tasks
- Processed today: X items
- Pending in Needs_Action: X items
- Awaiting your approval: X items

## Financial Snapshot
- Today's activity: <from Bank_Transactions>
- MTD Revenue: <from Accounting>

## Completed Tasks
<bullet list of Done/ items from today>

## Pending Approvals
<list of items in Pending_Approval/ needing attention>

## Proactive Suggestions
<any observations, cost savings, or flags based on Company Handbook rules>

---
*Generated by AI Employee v0.1*
```

7. After saving the briefing, update `Dashboard.md` Recent Activity with a link to the briefing.
